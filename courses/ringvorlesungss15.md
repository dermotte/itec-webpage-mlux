#### From OLAP to S-OLAP to I-OLAP

Postdoc-Ass. Dipl.-Ing. Dr. Christian Koncilia, ISYS, AAU

#### Abstract

During the last decades Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) became state of the art to analyze
huge data sets. OLAP systems are build to answer queries like „What are the sales for product P
in region R in March 2015“. However, existing OLAP techniques, although very advanced ones,
allow to analyze mostly set oriented data without exploiting the existing order among the data.
For this reason, it was necessary to create new models and techniques that would be able to
store and analyze such data efficiently. In this talk we will give a brief overview of a model
that enables the user to analyze both, sequential and interval based data, i.e. a model that
takes the order among data into account.

#### CV

Christian Koncilia is a postdoc at the University of Klagenfurt. Prior to this position, he was
a data warehousing consultant in Munich, Germany and a project manager in Klagenfurt, Austria.
He received his PhD in applied computer science in 2002. His research interests include temporal
databases, data warehousing and multidimensional databases. He published several papers in
international conference proceedings.

#### Effective Multimedia Content Dissemination in a Future Internet Architecture - the CONCERT Project

Univ.-Prof. Dipl.-Ing. Dr. Hermann Hellwagner, ITEC, AAU

#### Abstract

Multimedia content, in particular video, is the predominant traffic source in today's Internet.
Unfortunately, the Internet architecture and protocols have not been designed to cope with this
type of content. Thus, in the past decade, a variety of Future Internet architecture proposals
have come up, most importantly Information-Centric Networking (ICN) approaches. In ICN, the
network devices are designed to be content-aware and able to modify and cache content.

In this talk, we will present the objectives and our recent work and results of the
international basic research project CONCERT, which attempts to design, implement, and evaluate
mechanisms for effective multimedia content dissemination, most importantly video streaming, in
a widely investigated ICN architecture. The specific ICN architecture, forwarding techniques,
caching strategies, the use of scalably encoded video, and the use of context information to
support effective video streaming and, eventually, to achieve user satisfaction, will be
addressed.

#### CV

Hermann Hellwagner is a full professor of Informatics in the Institute of Information Technology
(ITEC), Alpen-Adria-Universität Klagenfurt, Austria, leading the Multimedia Communications
group. His current research areas are distributed multimedia systems, multimedia communications,
and quality of service. He has received many research grants from national (Austria, Germany)
and European funding agencies as well as from industry, is the editor of several books, and has
published more than 200 scientific papers on parallel computer architecture, parallel
programming, and multimedia communications and adaptation. He is a senior member of the IEEE,
member of the ACM, GI (German Informatics Society) and OCG (Austrian Computer Society), and Vice

#### Why do smart systems need telecommunications? Opportunities for advances in communication technology

Univ.-Prof. Dr. Andrea M. Tonello, NES, AAU

#### Abstract

There is a tremendous need for novel services in a number of market domains as energy, transportation, automotive that allow bettering the quality of life, increasing the user benefit and reducing the environmental impact. Systems are evolving into smart systems, or better to say, in systems that facilitate the user intelligent behavior. They are intrinsically becoming more complex and demanding the fulfillment of stringent requirements to the underlying enabling technologies which include the components (as energy generators, sensors/actuators, robots, vehicles), the communication infrastructures and the intelligence to smartly manage the interaction between such components. Despite the fact that communication technology (both wireless and wireline) has significant improved, new opportunities arise for the development of new solutions to overcome the resource limitations (spectrum and energy), the high traffic demands, the security/privacy issues. In wireless, this is known as 5G. In wireline, power line communications is gaining momentum and is fast evolving. In this talk, we will discuss some hot topics, challenges and approaches in wireless communication technology, as well as in power line communications, showing that despite they may appear as competitive technologies they are synergic and they can benefit from a convergent approach in many different application domains, e.g., in smart grids, for smart cars, in smart factories.

#### Linguistische Analysen von Geschäftsprozessmodellen

Dr. Agnes Koschmider, Karlsruher Institut für Technologie

#### Abstract

Zur Analyse des Verhaltens von Geschäftsprozessmodellen existieren sehr viele Methoden, die
theoretisch fundiert sind und in Werkzeugen des Geschäftsprozessmanagements praktisch eingesetzt
werden. Ein Geschäftsprozessmodell, welches ein korrektes Verhalten aufweist (z.B. in dem Sinn,
dass es verklemmungsfrei ist), kann für den potentiellen Nutzer trotzdem unbrauchbar sein, weil
er das Geschäftsprozessmodell inhaltlich nicht versteht. Die Frage nach linguistischen Analysen
von Geschäftsprozessmodellen wurde bislang wenig untersucht. Im Rahmen meiner Forschung
kombiniere ich Methoden der Linguistik, um neuartige Verfahren zur Transformation von
Geschäftsprozessmodellen in "verständlichere" Modelle zu unterstützen. Diese Verfahren werden im
Rahmen meines Vortrags vorgestellt.

#### CV

Dr. Agnes Koschmider ist Wissenschaftlerin am Karlsruher Institut für Technologie (Deutschland).
Im Juli 2007 promovierte sie im Fach der Angewandten Informatik mit der Arbeit
"Ähnlichkeitsbasierte Modellierungsunterstützung für Geschäftsprozessmodelle". Im Juli 2015 wird
sie im selben Fach habilitiert. In der Forschung beschäftigt sich Agnes Koschmider mit der
Entwicklung betrieblicher Informationssysteme. Insbesondere entwickelt sie Methoden zur
Unterstützung der Modellbildung. Seit 2013 ist Agnes Koschmider Junior-Fellow der Gesellschaft

#### Modeling of Solar Energy

Tamer T.N. Khatib, Ph.D, Senior Researcher, NES, AAU

#### Abstract

Solar radiation data provide information on how much of the sun’s energy strikes a surface at a
location on the earth during a particular time period. These data are needed for effective
research in solar-energy utilization. Due to the cost of and difficulty in solar radiation
measurements and these data are not readily available, alternative ways of generating these data
are needed. In this lecture, a review will be conducted on the solar energy modeling techniques
which are classified based on the nature of the modeling technique. Linear, nonlinear, satellite
based and artificial intelligence models for solar energy prediction hare considered.

#### Interactive Video Search

Assoc.-Prof. Dr. Klaus Schöffmann, ITEC, AAU

#### Abstract

With an increasing amount of video data in our daily life, the need for content-based search in
videos increases as well. Though a lot of research has been spent on video retrieval tools and
methods, which allow for automatic search in videos through content-based queries, still the
performance of automatic video retrieval is far from optimal. One problem of automatic video
retrieval is the fact that the actual search process is performed by the video retrieval engine,
which is a black box for the user. More importantly, there are situations where the common
query-and-browse-results approach cannot be employed, for example when users are not able to
formulate their search needs through a query - or when they simply want to browse the content
without any concrete query in mind. Interactive video search is an alternative method for
content-based search in videos. It provides flexible content interaction features and gives full
control of the search process to the user, who will know best which features to use and how, in
order to solve a search problem. In this talk I will discuss (i) proposed solutions for improved
video content navigation, (ii) typical interaction of content-based querying features, and (iii)
advanced video content visualization methods.

#### CV

Dr. Klaus Schöffmann is Associate Professor in the Distributed Multimedia Systems research group
at the Institute of Information Technology (ITEC) at Klagenfurt University, Austria. He received
his Ph.D. in Computer Science in 2009 and his Habilitation (venia docendi) in 2015, both from
Klagenfurt University. His research interests include interactive video search, video
processing, and multimedia systems and applications. He has co-authored more than 60
publications on various topics in multimedia and he has co-organized international conferences,
special sessions and workshops (e.g., MMM 2012, CBMI 2013, VisHMC 2014, MMC 2014, and MMC 2015).
He is co-founder of the Video Browser Showdown (VBS), an editorial board member of the Springer
International Journal on Multimedia Tools and Applications (MTAP), and a steering committee
member of the International Conference on MultiMedia Modelling (MMM). Additionally, he is member
of the IEEE and the ACM and a frequent reviewer for international conferences and journals in
the field of multimedia. Prof. Schoeffmann teaches various courses in computer science,
including mobile app developement (interactive multimedia applications), media technology,
distributed systems, distributed multimedia systems, and operating systems.
