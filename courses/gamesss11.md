# Computer Games

The goal of this course is to introduce students to computer game production. This is not necessarily a technical issue especially as game production involves mostly non-technical work like creative audio, 3D and visual design, product management and planning, storyboarding & writing, and testing.

Note that all content generated by the course participants goes to the [AAU Games Wiki](http://www.itec.uni-klu.ac.at/~mlux/aaugames/doku.php "http://www.itec.uni-klu.ac.at/~mlux/aaugames/doku.php").

# Assignments

## Assignment 1 - due 16.03

Play one of the following games for a *significant* amount of time. Those games were available as part of the [Humble Indie Bundles](http://www.humblebundle.com/ "http://www.humblebundle.com/").

* [Aquaria](http://www.bit-blot.com/aquaria/ "http://www.bit-blot.com/aquaria/")
* [Braid](http://www.braid-game.com/ "http://www.braid-game.com/")
* [Gish](http://www.chroniclogic.com/gish.htm "http://www.chroniclogic.com/gish.htm")
* [Osmos](http://www.hemispheregames.com/osmos/ "http://www.hemispheregames.com/osmos/")
* [World of Goo](http://www.worldofgoo.com/ "http://www.worldofgoo.com/")

Your task is to write an essay …

* telling me what you liked & disliked
* what you’d make better
* no more than 150 words

Put the essay on the [AAU Games Wiki](http://www.itec.uni-klu.ac.at/~mlux/aaugames/doku.php "http://www.itec.uni-klu.ac.at/~mlux/aaugames/doku.php"). Make sure you add your name and the game you played along with the essay.

## Assignment 2 - due 06.04

You are team member of a group, and you have your assignment (the paper strip giving you directions on the game). Your task is to

* gather with your team colleagues
* brain storm a game idea and concept
* write it down in a game concept document (e.g., one page MS Word 12pt font)
* publish it on the Wiki.

Put the concept document on the [AAU Games Wiki](http://www.itec.uni-klu.ac.at/~mlux/aaugames/doku.php "http://www.itec.uni-klu.ac.at/~mlux/aaugames/doku.php"). Make sure to add your group name, game title and the name of the team members.
