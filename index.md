# Assoc. Prof. Dr. DI Mathias Lux

**Associate Professor** | [Institute for Information Technology (ITEC)](https://www.itec.aau.at/) | University of Klagenfurt, Austria

Mathias Lux is an Associate Professor at the Institute for Information Technology (ITEC), University of Klagenfurt. His work bridges the gap between user intentions and multimedia systems, with a current focus on interdisciplinary research within **(video) games**.

### Research & Innovation
With [over 150 scientific publications](https://scholar.google.at/citations?user=ucLHfl4AAAAJ), Mathias has established a significant footprint in multimedia retrieval, social multimedia semantics, and interactive systems. He is well known for developing the award-winning open-source tools **Caliph & Emir** and **LIRE** (Lucene Image Retrieval). 

His work has high industry utility; he integrated image indexing features into the **Apache Solr** search server, a system that now powers global platforms such as the **WIPO Global Brand Database**. 

### Teaching & Academic Leadership
At the University of Klagenfurt, Mathias co-founded the Master’s program **Game Studies and Engineering**. He serves as the head of the curriculum committee and is a [regular lecturer](?data=courses.md) on computer graphics, procedural content generation, and game production. 

Beyond the classroom, he has fostered a vibrant local ecosystem of developers and enthusiasts through regular events and game jams in the Klagenfurt area. His commitment to the local game industry is further evidenced by his role as a scientific project advisor for the founding of [Dirty Paws Studio](https://www.dirtypaws.studio/) and [Fire Totem Games](https://www.firetotemgames.com/). He also contributed nearly a decade of service (2013–2022) as the Vice-Chair of the University Senate.

### Background & Professional Journey
Mathias earned his M.S. in Mathematics (2004) and his PhD in Telematics (2006) from Graz University of Technology, both with distinction. He received his Habilitation (*venia docendi*) from the University of Klagenfurt in 2013.

His career includes a mix of industry and academia, having worked on web applications for various companies and as a researcher at the *Know-Center* in Graz. He has served as an external lecturer for institutions including ENSEEHIT Toulouse, the University of Passau, and FH Kärnten, and has consulted for organizations like the United Nations. 

His reach extends to the creator economy; his most successful Android app, [Artist Grid](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=at.juggle.imagegrid), has surpassed 500k downloads, and his [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@dermotte) has reached over 230k views.
